# Coverage of events: competitions and single days, by sport and by day

Generated by `python3 -I pipeline/coverage_events.py`. Do not edit the numbers by hand.

The package holds **28,205 events and competition editions** with a day-precision date, of **185 sports**, from the Wikidata dump of 2026-10-12 (`wikidata-20261012-all.json.gz`, CC0, each with its Q id): 11,729 that began, 11,614 that ended and 4,862 that took place on one day; 5,951 name a winner and 19,204 a place, as Wikidata states them. An event is ranked by the number of Wikipedia editions of its page: everything with 20 or more, and below that the best two of each day, sport and kind with 5 or more. A date that is January 1 or December 31 is left out (a season is written that way), and so is an edition named for a season or a play-off, a league SPX's own archive holds, para, wheelchair, deaf and blind sport, and an edition whose end is before its start.

## By day

- Events per day: min 9, median 76, max 153 (the two edges of the year excepted).
- Sports per day: min 4, median 27, max 50.
- The quietest days are 12-24 (9), 12-30 (11), 12-27 (14), 12-25 (16), 12-26 (16).
- 01-01, 12-31 hold none: Wikidata writes a year's first and last day for a season or a year-only date, and the dump cannot tell them apart.

## The gaps

- **January 1 and December 31**, as said above.
- A competition whose page has fewer than five editions is not published, and one with fewer than 20 only when it is among the best two of its day and sport.
- A place or a winner is named only where the item states it; a winner that is a person is not named in an event whose name carries a sex marker (the event keeps its name, and no person is told to be of a sex), and a team is named by its country.
- A match, a stage or a bout is a fact only when the item holds a day and a name; the scores and the players of a game are not in these facts.
